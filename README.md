Web Developer and Designer
--------------------------

I'm a driven Web developer with an IT background and have a massive interest in design, creative roles and development. Since I finished my degree in IT and computer science with focus on web development and networking; I have been focusing on travelling.

After my degree in 2021, I got offered to work within education which I did for 3,5 years until I decided it was not what I was searching for. I wanted more freedom to explore the world. So for the last 1,5 years I have worked at a warehouse which have allowed me to travel much more and also continue coding independently on my free time.

But recently I have made the decision that it was time for me to transition back into development.

So I have created this GitHub and will upload my projects as I go. Recently I have made a concept website for a job application and will soon be uploading my portfolio as well, as I am working on that as we speak.

🌍  I'm based in Sweden
